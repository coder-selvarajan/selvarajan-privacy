# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 6, 2026**

Selvarajan Thangavel ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Pavitram mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information
- **Username and email address** — used for authentication and account identification.
- **Password** — securely hashed and stored; we do not have access to your plain-text password.
- **User role** — your assigned role (Admin, Purchase, or Sales) within your organization.

### Business Data
When using the App, you may enter business-related data including:
- Project details
- Vendor and customer names
- Purchase and sales bill information (amounts, dates, categories, GST details)
- Payment records

This data is entered voluntarily and is necessary for the App to function.

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Session tokens** — used to keep you signed in securely.

We do **not** collect:
- Location data
- Contacts
- Photos, camera, or microphone data
- Device identifiers for advertising
- Analytics or tracking data
- Cookies

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:
- Authenticate your identity and provide access to the App.
- Display and manage your business data (projects, bills, payments, reports).
- Generate financial reports and PDF documents within the App.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core functionality.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored securely on **Supabase** cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.
- Authentication credentials are managed through Supabase Auth with industry-standard security practices.
- Session tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage.
- We do not use any non-exempt encryption beyond standard HTTPS.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal or business data with third parties, except:
- **Supabase** — our cloud database and authentication provider, which processes data on our behalf. Supabase's privacy policy can be found at [https://supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy).

## 5. Data Retention

- Your data is retained as long as your account is active.
- If you wish to have your account and associated data deleted, please contact us at the email address below.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** — request a copy of your data.
- **Correction** — request corrections to inaccurate data.
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your account and data.
- **Portability** — request your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
